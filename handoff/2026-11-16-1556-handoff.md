## What the live checks decided

- Hidden card data, live updates, sign-in, card positions, setting and
  clearing a level on a card, and cover images (plain image tag) all work.
- Renaming or deleting fields and options is refused by Trello for OAuth 2.0
  tokens, so names are read-only in the matrix and changed in Trello's editor.
- Hidden card data cannot be read through REST, so there is no polling mode.
- Trello closes the matrix when a card opens; "Show on matrix" is the way back.
- Creating a field is not yet proven (a leftover test field got in the way).

## Known limits, left as they are

- Two people pressing Reorder or Undo at the same moment: the last one wins
  and the undo may be partial. The checklist says not to.
- A save that Trello answers later than 20 seconds is reported as failed; if
  it then lands after a newer save of the same card, the older spot wins, and
  an undo of a reorder made in between may skip that card. A guard (wait for
  the abandoned call before the next write of the same card) is known and not
  built.
- Whether Trello renews the sign-in by itself after an hour is unproven. If it
  does not, a bar "Trello sign-in has expired." with a Connect button appears.
  Acceptance line 16 tests this.
- The other person's page re-reads the board back to back while a long
  reorder runs. Acceptance line 17 watches for stutter.
